I am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Data Science at the University of Milano-Bicocca in Italy, having previously completed my undergraduate studies in Optoelectronic Information Science and Engineering at Guilin University of Electronic Technology.

My undergraduate education was highly interdisciplinary, covering a wide range of fields including mathematics, physics, electronic information, optics, and computer science. This background enables me to approach problems in data science and artificial intelligence from engineering, mathematical, and computational perspectives, while also providing a broad knowledge base for exploring various application domains.

During my undergraduate years, I maintained a strong interest in mathematics and physics, supplementing my formal coursework with self-study and winning multiple provincial-level awards in mathematics competitions. Subsequently, I shifted my academic focus toward artificial intelligence and data science; during my Master’s program, I have systematically studied subjects such as machine learning, statistical modeling, deep learning, optimization, time series analysis, natural language processing, and financial market analysis.(Since I have not graduated, some courses are not finished.)

Currently, I am particularly interested in the interdisciplinary application of AI methods to other fields. My primary interests lie in **machine learning**, **quantitative finance**, and **"AI for Science"**, as well as the mathematical and statistical techniques underpinning these areas. I aim to further explore how data-driven and machine learning approaches can be leveraged to solve problems rooted in specific scientific or real-world contexts.
